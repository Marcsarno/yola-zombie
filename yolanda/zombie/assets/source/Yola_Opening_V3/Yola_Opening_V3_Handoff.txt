Card 1 — Assessment: history changes the plan
Chart: scheduled knee procedure; general anesthesia is planned. Prior testing confirms butyrylcholinesterase (pseudocholinesterase) deficiency. Routine history, medications, allergies, fasting, consent and relevant preparation are documented; the short conversation is not a complete preanesthesia examination.
Prompt: “Which pair should be avoided because of his documented enzyme deficiency?”

B. Rocuronium and vecuronium
C. Propofol and sevoflurane
Success: “History accounted for. An alternative plan is selected.”
Explanation on demand: the affected pair can cause prolonged paralysis in this condition. Today's clinician-selected alternative uses rocuronium, which is not dependent on that enzyme.
Retry: “Check which two muscle relaxants depend on this enzyme.”
No incorrect drug is administered. The diagnosis is documented, not guessed from ‘slow to wake.’

Card 2 — Equipment: prepare to measure recovery
Prompt: “Which monitoring will best confirm neuromuscular recovery at the end of the case?”

B. Quantitative monitoring at the adductor pollicis — CORRECT
C. A sustained head-lift test alone
Success: “Quantitative recovery monitor ready.”
Retry: “Clinical or visual checks alone can miss residual block.”
This is an anticipatory equipment choice before anesthesia, not an improvised test after a crisis.

Card 3 — Monitor: interpret the result
Context: after rocuronium, four twitch responses are present. Quantitative TOF ratio: 0.78.
Prompt: “Does this establish adequate neuromuscular recovery for extubation?”
A. Yes. Four responses establish full recovery.
B. Yes, if a head lift can be sustained.
C. No. Residual neuromuscular blockade remains. — CORRECT
Success: “Recovery checks continue.”
Explanation: confirm a quantitative TOF ratio of at least 0.9 alongside the other clinical readiness requirements before extubation. A TOF ratio alone is not the complete readiness assessment.
Retry: “Four responses do not establish full recovery. Check the ratio.”
Continue appropriate care, then show 0.96 and other criteria satisfied before handoff.
