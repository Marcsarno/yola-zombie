# Handoff for the PC chat: the story reset, Chapter 2 version 3 (written 2026-10-22)

Gameplay beats only. Dialogue is described by what it does, not written out: a new voice script will follow once Marc locks
the beats. **This replaces every earlier version of this file and the Chapter 2 parts of story/NOTES.md.** Marc says "apply
the design" to use it. PROPOSED = Claude's suggestion, Marc hasn't said yes. OPEN = undecided.

## The reset
The opening reveal (hospital sim -> zombie game) stays as the fun jump. Everything after it is a new story: no baseball
player, no patient, no specimen case, no record match.
- A rare **vaccine** (Marc calls it "the cure"; Yola, "the vaccine") prevents infection. It does **not** cure a bite
  ("it only works before"). The hospital is overrun, so there will be no more.
- **Four doses** are hidden at the family's house. Three are for Grammy, Lilah and Arianna. Marc saves the fourth for Yola
  and won't take it until she has one too. Yola says she'll find more.
- Lilah, Grammy and Arianna are already at Grammy's (Chapter 4). In Chapter 2 Marc is **alone**.
- Chapters alternate: 1 Floor 7 (Yola), 2 Marc's neighborhood, 3 Floor 6 (Yola), 4 Grammy's neighborhood (Marc), 5 Floor 5 (Yola).

## Chapter 2, beat by beat (Marc's neighborhood)

1. **Load and sneak.** Black, the chapter title, then Marc crouched behind a barricade at the edge of the neighborhood,
   sneaking in (a low walk). He starts with the **pistol, no silencer**. A short **camera look** shows two armed looters far
   off, then returns to Marc. A whispered call: Marc says he has no idea how he'll get out of here with this; Yola says
   figure it out, the family is counting on you. Yola says the vials are in the **second fridge in the garage**.
2. **Overheard.** Cars hide him. Looters talk about searching freezers, "anything cold," medicine hidden in hospital
   people's houses, going down a list, and that James is at the end house with his headphones on. No stealth system: they
   never notice him on the way in.
3. **The beacon.** Tinny music leaks from the end house. The door is ajar.
4. **The house.** James is going through the **kitchen fridge** (the wrong one). He turns on Marc; Marc shoots; **the camera
   dips and the head comes off.** Marc, over him: "Sorry, buddy. Wrong fridge." The garage door is next to the kitchen. At
   the garage fridge: "Behind the beer. Smart." The vials are in a **silver cold-chain case** with a hospital symbol and a
   "x4" label. Marc carries it in one hand and fights with a one-handed weapon.
5. **The rescue.** Stepping outside he hears shots and screams from the southeast. A **short camera look** at her front yard:
   her husband, firing at zombies, is overwhelmed and bitten. No looters. A suitcase and an open trunk show they were
   leaving; his shooting drew the zombies. Marc runs over and clears a small bounded wave (pistol only, case in hand).
   The husband says "Hey. I've got you." and turns in Sophie's arms (use the turning textures). Marc shoots him. Sophie uses
   the frightened animation throughout.
6. **The reveal.** Marc lowers the case to the grass. Camera: Sophie looking at Marc, then the case, then a slow **zoom on
   the symbol** (the "x4"), then her line: is it what she thinks it is? Marc explains: **it only works before.** She asks
   how he got here, and says she has a **boat, but it's out of gas**. Marc refuses: he's only here for his own family. She
   pleads: she's alone; help her get past the horde and he can use the boat. He agrees. She **does not ask for a vial.**
   Instead, **glance #1**: she looks at the case a beat too long when he picks it up.
7. **The radio.** The radio on Marc's belt shows a **blinking red battery light** (the player notices it). Sophie says "Come
   this way."
8. **The gate.** It is locked. Sophie **picks the lock** ("I got this"). Marc: "When did you learn that?" She doesn't
   answer. While she works, she says he'll want something quiet. A **busted-open house just left of the gate** shows a
   **crowbar** and **batteries** inside, visible from the start.
9. **The busted house.** One zombie inside gives the **crowbar** its first kill. The crowbar is one-handed and carried on his
   back. The batteries stop the red light. The radio comes back and **Yola calls**: she lost him and is relieved. Marc tells
   her about the boat and the survivor who saw the case. She says to **watch her.** They renew the pact: the fourth is
   Yola's, and she'll find more. **Order-proof:** if the player already has the crowbar or batteries, Sophie's lines are
   shorter or skipped; nothing ever blocks progress.
10. **The walk.** A short bounded **pistol** fight on the way to the clubhouse.
11. **The clubhouse.** Sophie says there are supplies and warns: **don't use your gun**; it'll wake the horde. **Camera
    look:** about 30 zombies at the playground. **Melee only** (the crowbar) from here until the silencer is found. Only
    zombies inside; **no looters.** Marc sets the case down to swing two-handed: **glance #2**, Sophie's hand drifts toward
    it, he turns, she pulls it back. He finds supplies and a **silencer**. Sophie: "It's not in here. He must have it on
    him." Marc: "Who has what?" She doesn't answer. "Just follow me."
12. **The parking lot.** Sophie runs ahead (she seems to know where). "Help! Help!" Pete is being killed by zombies. Marc
    kills them with the **silenced pistol**; the spot is far from the playground so the horde doesn't hear. Sophie takes the
    key from Pete's body.
13. **The dock.** Her boat is a pull-start outboard, nearly dry. The camera **lingers on the cooler** (the plant). Sophie
    asks for the case: "There's a cooler on the boat. I have no gas. It's locked. I need your help. Grab the gas so we can
    go. Please." Marc hesitates (looks at the case, at her), then hands it over. **Glance #3.** She hands him the key at
    last: it's the shed's. (The shed has a heavy disc padlock she can't pick.) He heads to the shed.
14. **The shed** (moved next to the boat; see "Map"). He takes the **gas can** and sees the **shotgun** for the first time (a
    beat shows it). Then, from the boat: a **pull cord, a cough, an engine that roars for a few seconds and dies.** Camera:
    the horde at the playground hears it and **runs, fast.**
15. **Sophie.** Cut to the boat: about **six zombies** surround her on the ground and attack (we hear her). Cut back to Marc.
16. **The fight.** Marc runs back and kills them all with the **shotgun.** The wider horde (about 30) reaches him in two or
    three **bounded waves**; plenty of shells; a brief **slow-motion beat on the first blast only.**
17. **After.** Silence. A held, silent shot of Marc over her. The cooler is open and empty, and the case is in her arms or at
    her feet (the betrayal reads at a glance). He picks up the case. PROPOSED closing line: "You could have come with us."
18. **Leaving.** He fuels the boat with the gas, pulls the cord (it starts on the first pull), and leaves. A last short call
    to Yola: he's leaving the dock, the survivor didn't make it, he still has all four, the fourth is still hers.

## Principles for this chapter
- **Marc never says "Grammy" or "Mom"**: he says "the family" or "them." The kids call her Grammy (that tells her apart from Babcia), so Grammy is fine in the kids' lines and as the Chapter 4 label.
- **Order-proof:** every optional pickup or conversation must still work if the player does it early, late or never.
- **One camera trick per moment:** a pan for the looters and the horde; a dip for James; slow motion for the first shotgun
  blast; a held silent shot over Sophie. The camera is otherwise fixed.
- **No stealth system.** The sneaking is staged. The only noise rule: an unsilenced shot near the clubhouse, or the engine,
  wakes the horde.
- **Bounded waves,** every arena sealed, no arrows (the world guides: the music, the blinking light, the glow, Sophie).
