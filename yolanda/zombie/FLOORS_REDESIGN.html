Floors 7 / 6 / 5: redesign plan

Proposal for review (2026-10-18, combat chat). North is the top: the back wall the locked camera sees. Every floor uses Floor 7's footprint (48 × 39 m) and keeps the lift on the top wall at the same spot. Floor 7 stays as it is, plus a few additions. Floors 6 and 5 are new: nine rooms each, with corridors, toilets and a janitor closet. Nothing is built yet.

The rules every floor follows

  • Hospital corridors, but you can't skip the rooms. Each floor has a corridor you can see, cut by a fire shutter (outbreak lockdown) or a collapsed ceiling. The route goes through the rooms. A shutter only opens from its far side, so it becomes the quick way back (a loop, never a skip).
  • Cracked walls break to anything heavy: the sledgehammer, the big lady's charge, the guard's slam. One rule, taught three ways. A cracked wall shows a big jagged crack with dust trickling from it.
  • At least one toilet and one janitor closet on every floor. They're pockets for loot and a scare, and the doors zombies burst out of (spawns with a reason).
  • One room per floor is the "heart": a nurse station or reception with its desk in the middle, built for circling fights.

Floor 7: Restore the way down keep, with three additions

The rooms, route and story stay as they are now. Added:

  • Staff toilets, off Endoscopy and Sterile Supply: the first room after the opening, so its first scare is a zombie banging inside a stall.
  • The janitor closet as its own room: the mop sink, the cart, the linen. It moves out of Lift Plant, which keeps its open bay as the lady's lane.
  • The first cracked wall, and the sledgehammer right before it: the Refurbishment's doorway into the canteen is bricked up with temporary blockwork, cracked. The sledgehammer lies beside it. Smash through.
  • The Equipment Store keeps its optional powered door from the canteen.
  • The thin gap between the Recovery Ward and the Equipment Store becomes a blocked service corridor (a gurney barricade): it reads as a hospital and stays shut.

Floor 6: Cold dispatch new: the surgical ward floor

The idea: a real ward floor. A main corridor runs across it, a fire shutter and a collapsed ceiling cut it into three, and the nurse station sits in the middle of the floor.

What's new here: oxygen tanks (the user's pick):

  • The wheelchair zombies carry an oxygen cylinder on the back of the chair and wheel themselves at Yola quickly.
  • Older walkers drag a cylinder on a little trolley.
  • Loose cylinders stand in the corridor and the cylinder store.
  • Shoot a cylinder and it bursts: a short hiss, then a blast that throws or kills every zombie around it and sets off other tanks nearby. Yola is hurt if she's too close.

Shoot them early, in the middle of a crowd. Let one roll up to you and it's your problem.

The way there (always open)

  • Lift Lobby → the corridor's east end (the toilets and janitor closet are off it) → the shutter blocks the way west.
  • Ward 6B Surgical: a wide ward and the first fight. A contractor's trolley holds a sledgehammer (the player's second use of one). The door west is jammed and the wall next to it is cracked: smash through.
  • On-call and Doctors' Offices (desks, a couch, lockers: a tight fight) → the corridor's west end (dead-ended by the collapsed ceiling) → Bay 6A Orthopaedic (traction frames to circle) → Cold Dispatch: the sealed case.

The other approach (the lady)

  • The corridor → the Sample Prep Lab (glass wall: you can see into the nurse station) → Sterile Services → Ward 6C Conversion Works: the big lady. Dodge her charge into its cracked west wall and she breaks straight through into Cold Dispatch. Then she's down: finisher.

The way back (new ground, then the shortcut)

  • Cold Dispatch → Bay 6A → the Nurse Station, the floor's heart: the central desk to circle, and a fight you haven't seen yet. → the middle of the corridor → raise the fire shutter from this side → the lobby.

Floor 5: Match the sample new: the MRI arena and the flood

The idea: one loop through the floor, every room new. The atrium hole is dropped (the user). The middle of the floor is the Main Reception: a big waiting hall with the round desk in the middle.

The loop (either direction)

  • Lift Lobby → Diagnostic Intake (beside the lift: the case is secured here) → Main Reception.
  • The Isolation Ward: flooded.
    • Burst pipes have left the floor under ankle-deep water, which slows everyone.
    • A sparking electrical panel on the wall: hit it (shoot it or strike it) and the water carries a jolt that fries every zombie standing in it. Yola is hurt too if she's in it.
    • It recharges after a while. Lure them in, step out, zap.
  • Medical Records: the record.
  • Imaging: the big lady's 33 m straight lane.
  • The MRI Arena (the user's pick):
    • The magnet comes alive as Yola walks in, with a deep hum and a blue glow. It rips her gun out of her hands and pins it to the machine, so she fights this room hand to hand.
    • Loose metal (drip stands, trays, wheelchairs) gets dragged across the floor and slams into the zombies in its way.
    • The guard is here: his shield is torn off his arm and stuck to the magnet. He staggers, open, and the gold mark comes up at once: a finisher without waiting for his slam.
    • Room cleared: hit the big red emergency shutdown button. Real MRIs dump their helium when shut down that way, so a huge white cloud blasts out of the vent and the magnet dies. Her gun drops; pick it up.
  • Radiology Reading → back to Intake: the ID match (the chapter payoff), take the case, the lift.

Optional

  • The Pathology Lab behind a cracked wall in the flooded ward (the sledgehammer, or a charge): loot.
  • Public toilets off the reception, staff toilets and the janitor closet off the Isolation Ward.
