decor3: new props from the Endoscopy, PreOp and General OR clinical runtime packs (art, signs, glove dispenser,
sharps container, biohazard bin). Pack models that were already in the project (handwash set, sanitizer, headwall,
surgical light, recovery furniture) are reused from their existing folders. Wall pieces use the wall-face-at-floor
origin with the mounting height baked in; the handwash fittings need height offsets added at placement.
